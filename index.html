How do I get my NPSSO?
  1. Open a new tab and sign in at playstation.com.
  2. In that same tab, go to ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie.
  3. You'll see {"npsso":"..."} — copy the long value between the quotes.
  4. Paste it above and tap Connect.

This token is as sensitive as your password — only paste it here, never share it.
